4. Age, children and adolescents

  • The first time you try to write to other players, the game asks whether you are 18 or older or under 18. The answer can't be changed from the game afterwards.
  • Players under 18 may only use the online service with the permission of a parent or other legal guardian, who also accepts these terms and is responsible for how the account is used.
  • Children under 12 may only have an account created or authorised by their parent or guardian, who gives the consent required by art. 14 of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei 13.709/2018).
  • Chat off by default: as required by art. 20 of Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Lei 15.211/2025), every way of writing to other players (the lounge chat, the match chat, private messages, gift messages, the profile's status message and the "looking for a game" note) starts switched off for everyone, and while it is off you don't see what others write there either. Players 18 or older switch it on by giving their age group (section 3: it must be true). On accounts of players under 18, only the parent or guardian switches it on, in the game itself, with a declaration; the game keeps the date of that permission, and the parent or guardian can switch everything off again at any time (the "For a parent or guardian" button in the lounge's chat). Ready-made reactions, invitations, friends and games stay available.
  • Private messages: players under 18 exchange private messages with their friends only. Between players 18 or older who aren't friends, the writer sends at most 3 messages until they get an answer and starts at most 10 new conversations a day; each player chooses, in Settings → Game, whether players who aren't their friends may write to them, and can block or report anyone who bothers them.
  • Purchases with money made by players under 18 need the parent's or guardian's permission. The parent or guardian may ask for a refund as explained in section 8.
  • There are no loot boxes: nothing paid for in the game is random. Every item has a fixed price, shown before you buy it.
  • Parents and guardians can contact us (section 1) to ask about a minor's account, have it deleted or report a problem. We may suspend an account that seems to belong to a child without a parent's or guardian's permission.

6. Reports, sanctions and appeals

To report someone, use the report button on their profile or during the game; the reported player's latest messages go with the report for the staff to review. You can also block any player: they can no longer write to you, invite you or see you.

Depending on how serious and repeated it is, the staff may:

  • send a warning;
  • delete messages, the profile's status message or notes;
  • mute the player (15 minutes to 30 days: they play, but can't write);
  • change a username that breaks the rules;
  • suspend the account (from 1 day to permanently), showing the reason and how long when they sign in;
  • take away coins, items, rating or prizes obtained by cheating, a bug or fraud.

Cases that may be crimes, above all against children and adolescents, are reported to the authorities, and the data needed for that is kept. If you think a sanction was unfair, write to the contact in section 1: we review the decision and answer within 5 business days.

8. Purchases with money and refunds

What is sold: coin packs, the welcome pack (once per account: exclusive items and coins), the monthly VIP subscription (section 9) and a month of VIP as a gift for a friend. What each one includes is shown in the game before you buy.

How you pay:

  • In the version downloaded from the website or GitHub, you pay on Stripe's secure page, which opens in your browser (card, Pix or boleto, depending on what is available). Payment details stay with Stripe; the game and the server never receive them.
  • In the Microsoft Store version, coin packs are bought through the Store itself, which charges you and follows Microsoft's terms and refund policy.

Price: the final price, with its currency (for example, R$), is shown on the payment page before you pay. There are no hidden charges.

Delivery: coins, items and VIP reach your account as soon as the payment is confirmed: at once for card and Pix, and usually within 3 business days for boleto. If something hasn't arrived 24 hours after the confirmation, contact us.

Right of withdrawal (7 days)

As guaranteed by art. 49 of Brazil's Consumer Defence Code, you may withdraw from any purchase made outside the Microsoft Store within 7 days of the purchase, without giving a reason:

  • write to the contact in section 1 with your username and the date and amount of the purchase (or the receipt);
  • we refund the full amount paid, by the same means of payment (on a card, the refund shows on one of your next statements, depending on the bank);
  • with the refund, everything the purchase gave is taken back: the coins (if they were already spent, the balance goes below zero and nothing can be bought with coins until it is positive again), the welcome pack's exclusive items and VIP.

After 7 days there is no refund for a change of mind, but you keep every right under the Consumer Defence Code: if a purchase was not delivered, was charged twice or doesn't work as promised, we fix it or give the money back.

Microsoft Store: purchases made through the Store are refunded by Microsoft, under its policy. If something bought through the Store doesn't reach the game, contact us as well.

Disputed payments (chargebacks): before disputing a purchase with your bank, contact us: within 7 days the refund is made with no questions asked. A purchase that is disputed or refunded by the bank has what it gave taken back as above, and disputes made to keep items without paying may lead to the account's suspension.

9. VIP subscription

  • What it gives: coins every paid month, a crown by your name, items and reactions only for members, every replay kept and the other perks for looks or comfort listed on the shop's VIP tab. VIP doesn't help win games.
  • Renewal: the subscription is monthly and renews by itself, with the same means of payment, until cancelled. The amount and the date of the next charge are shown in the subscription portal.
  • Cancelling: any time, in the game: Shop → VIP → Manage subscription, which opens Stripe's portal. VIP lasts until the end of the month already paid, and there are no further charges. The monthly coins already received stay with you; VIP items stay in your collection and can be used again if you subscribe again.
  • Withdrawal: the subscription's first charge can be refunded in full within 7 days (section 8). After that there is no partial refund of the current month: cancelling only stops the next charges.
  • Price changes: if the price of VIP changes, we announce it in the game at least 30 days before the renewal at the new price, and you can cancel before then.
  • VIP as a gift: a one-off payment for one month (31 days) that doesn't renew.
  • Deleting the account cancels its VIP subscription.

Coins and items have no money value, but purchases made in the 7 days before the announcement can still be refunded. The modes that don't use the server keep working.

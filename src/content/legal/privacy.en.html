This policy explains what data the game {{GAME}} and its online server keep, what for, who it is shared with and how you can exercise your rights, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Lei 13.709/2018) and Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Lei 15.211/2025). It is part of the terms of use.

Who is responsible

  • Tax ID (CPF/CNPJ): {{DOCUMENT}}
  • Address: {{ADDRESS}}
  • Contact and data protection officer: {{CONTACT}}

The controller also acts as the person in charge of personal data processing (art. 41 of the LGPD): every request about your data goes to the contact above.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data.
  • We show no ads, build no advertising profiles and use no third-party tracking or analytics tools. The game's pages use no cookies.
  • Card and payment details never pass through the game or the server.
  • We don't ask players for their real name, date of birth, phone number, address, photo or documents.

Playing without an account

Games against the computer (including the Tour and the tournaments against the computer), two players on one computer and games on a local network send nothing to the online server while you are not signed in. On a local network, the name you type and the strokes go straight to the other computers in the game. Settings, the history of local games and medals from tournaments against the computer not yet sent stay on your computer only.

Outside the Microsoft Store and Flathub, the game asks GitHub whether there is a new version; that request carries no data from your account.

Data kept when you create an account and play online

  • Account: username, country (if you choose one), the profile picture chosen from the game's own, the profile's status message (if you write one), the creation date, how many times the name was changed and the password, stored only as an Argon2 hash (nobody can read it).
  • Age group: whether you are 18 or older or under 18, when you give it to switch the chat on. On accounts of minors, also whether the parent or guardian allowed the chat and the date of that permission.
  • Recovery e-mail (optional): only if you give it, to prove the account is yours if you forget the password.
  • IP address: used for a few minutes, in the server's memory, to limit abuse (password attempts, mass accounts, too many messages). The IP of the sign-up is kept with the account, to investigate mass accounts and fraud.
  • Session: an access code to sign in automatically. The server keeps only a hash of it; the code is in the settings file on your computer.
  • Presence: whether you are online and what you are doing (in the lounge, at a table, playing, in focus mode), visible to other players while you are online; when you were last seen (shown to friends); the days you played and, for one week, the hours you were online, for the staff's metrics.
  • Game: games (opponent, result, the rules used and the strokes, for replays), rating, experience and level, statistics and the analysis of your shots (by pocket, distance and kind, which only you see, as a VIP member), achievements, missions, your daily challenge and drill results, tournaments, medals from tournaments against the computer, the Tour's final, daily games, each cue's wins, friends, friend requests and blocks, gifts (with their message, if any) and "looking for a game" notes.
  • Ghost: when someone plays a player's ghost, the server works out that player's style from their recent games and sends only aggregated numbers (for example, the potting rate); the result stays in memory for up to 30 minutes and is not stored.
  • Chat: lounge and match messages stay only in the server's memory (the most recent ones) and are not stored. When someone is reported, the reported player's latest messages are attached to the report for the moderators to review.
  • Private messages: messages between two players (the text, who sent it, to whom, when, and whether it was read) are stored on the server, so they reach players who are offline and the conversation goes on on any computer. Each conversation keeps its 500 most recent messages, and only the two people in it see them in the game.
  • Settings: the game's settings that follow the account (aiming, camera, keys, language, muted players, who may send you private messages and others), so the game plays the same on another computer. Graphics, volume and interface size stay on your computer only.
  • Moderation: reports, warnings, mutes, suspensions, staff notes and the log of every staff action.
  • Shop: coins, items, coin history and, for purchases with money made outside the Microsoft Store, the payment record (what was bought, amount, currency, date, Stripe's identifiers and the e-mail given at checkout). For purchases made through the Microsoft Store, the pack received, the date and the purchase and order identifiers given by Microsoft; until the purchase is confirmed, also the Microsoft Store key that identifies it (deleted afterwards). The server receives no card details and no data about your Microsoft account.

What we use it for, and the legal basis

  • Running the online game (accounts, games, ranking, friends, tournaments, the shop, replays): performance of the contract you accept when creating the account (art. 7, V, of the LGPD).
  • Keeping the game safe and fair (limiting abuse, investigating reports and cheating, enforcing the rules, usage metrics): legitimate interest (art. 7, IX), always limited to what is needed.
  • Protecting children and adolescents (age group, chat off by default, the parent's or guardian's permission, private messages between friends only): legal obligation (art. 7, II, and art. 14 of the LGPD; art. 20 of Lei 15.211/2025) and the parent's or guardian's consent.
  • Meeting legal and tax obligations about purchases and complying with orders from authorities: legal obligation (art. 7, II) and the regular exercise of rights (art. 7, VI).

Children and adolescents

  • The game asks the age group the first time a player tries to write to other players. Players under 18 play online with a parent's or guardian's permission, and children under 12 only with an account created or authorised by the parent or guardian, who gives the consent of art. 14 of the LGPD.
  • The chat and the other ways of writing to other players start switched off for everyone. Players 18 or older switch them on by giving their age group; on accounts of players under 18, only the parent or guardian switches them on, in the game itself; the date of that permission is kept, and the parent or guardian can switch everything off again at any time. Players under 18 exchange private messages with their friends only: no one else can write to them.
  • Children's and adolescents' data is handled in their best interest, only so that the game works safely: no ads, no advertising profiles and no sharing beyond what is described here. There are no loot boxes in the game.
  • Parents and guardians can contact us to ask for access to the minor's data, its correction or the account's deletion.

Who we share it with

Only with those who provide services that make the game work, and only what is needed:

  • Hosting for the server and the database (cloud providers, which may be outside Brazil, for example in the United States).
  • Stripe, for purchases with money made outside the Microsoft Store. You enter your payment details directly on Stripe's page, under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft, when the game is installed or bought through the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's own privacy policy. To confirm purchases made through the Microsoft Store, the server sends Microsoft a player identifier that doesn't reveal who you are.
  • Authorities, when the law or a court order requires it, or to report possible crimes, above all against children and adolescents.

Other players see your name, country, profile picture, status message, rating, level, statistics, achievements and match history on your profile and in the ranking, and see your online presence as described above. The moderators see what they need to moderate; only administrators see e-mail addresses and payments.

International transfer

The servers may be outside Brazil, for example in the United States. The transfer is made to perform the contract with you (art. 33, IX, of the LGPD) and with providers that use contractual clauses and security measures to protect the data.

How long we keep it

  • Account data, for as long as the account exists. Sessions expire after 30 days unused.
  • Private messages, while both accounts of the conversation exist (the 500 most recent of each conversation).
  • The hours you were online, for one week. Abuse counters by IP, for a few minutes.
  • Purchase records, for the period required by tax law (usually 5 years).
  • Reports and moderation records, while they are needed for the game's safety or to defend rights, including in legal proceedings.

Security

Passwords and session codes are stored only as hashes, the connection to the server is encrypted, the staff's access to data is limited by their role (moderator or administrator) and every staff action is logged. If a security incident happens that may cause significant risk or harm, we will tell those affected and Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), as the law requires.

Your rights and how to delete your account

The LGPD (art. 18) gives you the right to: confirm whether we process your data and access it; correct incomplete or wrong data; ask for unnecessary or excessive data to be anonymised, blocked or deleted; portability; know who we share it with; be told about the possibility of not consenting and withdraw consent; and object to processing that doesn't follow the law. To exercise these rights, write to the contact above with your username; we answer within 15 days. You may also complain to the ANPD.

In the game itself, you can change your country and recovery e-mail and delete your account: Profile → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate: the name, password, country, e-mail, IP, age group, sessions, friends, blocks, achievements, medals, the settings kept with the account and private messages (sent and received) are deleted. Games already played stay in the opponents' history, linked to an anonymous account, and purchase records are kept without the e-mail, for the period the law requires.

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. Important changes are also announced in the game.
